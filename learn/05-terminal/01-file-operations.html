5.1 File operations >_

In Windows Explorer you right-click. In cmd you copy and del. In bash you cp and rm. Same ideas, shorter words — and everything is case-sensitive now: Notes.txt and notes.txt are two different files.

💡 Windows refugee tip

There's no C:\ drive. Disks and partitions are mounted into one tree starting at / ("root"). Your files live in /home/<you>, shown as ~.

⚠️ Watch out

rm does not use a Recycle Bin. Deleting is permanent — Linux assumes you meant it. (In a real shell, rm -i asks first.)
